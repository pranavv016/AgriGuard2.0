About AgriGuard 🌱

A smart, free agricultural information and planning platform that helps farmers identify crop problems, understand soil, estimate yield, track farming profit, and know market prices — all in one place.

What is AgriGuard?

AgriGuard is a smart agriculture platform that brings together the information farmers need most: details on crop and flower diseases, guidance on soil conditions, expected yield, farming profitability, and market price trends. Instead of visiting different places, a farmer can see all of this information from one spot.

This platform is built around real farming decisions. Before sowing, a farmer wants to know whether the soil is ready. While the crop is growing, symptoms of disease need to be spotted early. Before harvest, they want to estimate how much yield they'll get. After harvest, they need to know whether the season was profitable, and what price the market is offering. AgriGuard is organized around these very stages — and each page is focused on its own job.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make essential agricultural information simple, accessible, and free for every farmer — regardless of farm size, crop type, or location. We believe that better information, delivered in simple language, leads to better decisions in the field and better outcomes at harvest.

What You Can Do on AgriGuard

  • Learn about crop and flower diseases — see information on common diseases affecting grains, pulses, oilseeds, vegetables, fruits, and flower crops on the Diseases page.
  • Check a possible disease from a photo or symptoms — on the Disease Predictor, add a photo of your crop or select the symptoms you're seeing to get an educational, indicative match.
  • Check soil condition — on the Soil Predictor, enter a photo or basic readings and get a simple-language summary of soil health along with suitable crop suggestions.
  • Calculate expected yield — use the Yield Calculator to estimate how much yield (in quintals) your land is likely to produce.
  • Calculate farming profit — in the Profit Calculator, enter what you spent and what you expect to earn, and see the estimated profit or loss.
  • View market prices — on the Market Prices page, see indicative, regularly-updated prices for major crops and flowers.

Crop & Flower Disease Information

Diseases can reduce both the quality and quantity of a crop, sometimes drastically. AgriGuard provides educational information on common diseases affecting grains like wheat and rice, cash crops like cotton and sugarcane, vegetables like tomato, potato, and brinjal, fruits like mango, citrus, pomegranate, and grapes, spices like turmeric and ginger, and flowers like rose and marigold.

This information is meant for awareness and early identification only. It is not a diagnosis. Farmers should always consult a qualified agriculture officer or extension worker before making any treatment decision. A photo added in the Disease Predictor is also for record and reference only; the final suggestion is based on the symptoms you select.

Soil Health Awareness

Soil pH, moisture, and nutrient levels (nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium) play a big role in deciding which crop will do well. AgriGuard's soil check turns a few basic readings into a simple-language summary — whether the soil is acidic, alkaline, or balanced, whether moisture is adequate, and which crops are typically suited to that type of soil.

Farm Economics — Yield & Profit

Knowing how much yield a field is likely to produce, and how much that yield will sell for, is essential to good farm planning. AgriGuard's calculators let a farmer estimate total expected yield from a land area, and then work out estimated profit or loss once expenses, yield, and selling price are entered.

Market Awareness

Crop and flower prices change constantly based on supply, demand, weather, and local market conditions. AgriGuard shows indicative mandi prices for major crops and flowers in a simple table that updates every time the site is used, so farmers can track general price trends before deciding to sell.

Disease Library

Major Crop & Flower Diseases

Learn about common diseases affecting agricultural crops, vegetables, fruits, spices, and flowers. If you see symptoms, visit the Disease Predictor for an indicative match.

🍅
Tomato

Early Blight

A common fungal disease affecting tomatoes and related crops. Dark spots and lesions can develop on the leaves.

Warm and humid conditions can encourage this disease.

🌾
Wheat

Wheat Rust

Rust disease can produce orange-brown or rust-colored pustules on wheat plants.

Severe infections can affect plant growth and grain production.

🌱
Rice

Rice Blast

Rice blast is a fungal disease that can create lesions on leaves and other parts of the plant.

Severe infection can cause significant yield loss.

🥔
Potato

Late Blight

Late blight can create dark lesions on the leaves and stems of potato and tomato plants.

Cool, wet, and humid conditions can help it spread.

🍇
Multiple crops

Powdery Mildew

Powdery mildew creates a white, powdery coating on the surface of plants.

It can affect many agricultural and horticultural crops, including grapes and mango.

🫘
Pulses

Downy Mildew

Under favorable conditions, downy mildew can create yellow or pale spots on leaves.

High humidity and prolonged leaf wetness help it develop.

🌾
Rice

Bacterial Leaf Blight

A bacterial disease that creates water-soaked streaks on leaves, which later turn yellow and dry out, often with wilting.

It spreads quickly in warm, humid weather and standing water.

🌿
Cotton & Banana

Fusarium Wilt

A soil-borne fungal disease that blocks water movement inside the plant, causing wilting and yellowing even when there's enough water.

Infected plants often show soft rot near the base of the stem.

🍊
Citrus fruits

Citrus Canker

A bacterial disease that creates raised, water-soaked spots on the leaves, stems, and fruit of citrus trees.

Wind-driven rain and insect damage can help it spread between trees.

🥭
Mango, chilli & grapes

Anthracnose

A fungal disease that creates dark, sunken lesions on the leaves, fruit, and flowers of many fruit and vegetable crops.

Humid weather during flowering and fruiting increases the risk.

🌱
Cotton

Cotton Leaf Curl Virus

A virus spread by whitefly that causes leaves to curl upward, thicken, and show stunted, twisted growth.

Controlling whitefly numbers helps limit its spread.

🌹
Rose & marigold

Botrytis Blight (Gray Mold)

A fungal disease that creates gray, fuzzy mold on flower buds, petals, and stems, often after cool, humid weather.

Good air circulation and removing spent flowers can reduce its spread.

🥜
Groundnut

Tikka Disease (Leaf Spot)

Creates dark brown to black round spots on leaves, which can cause premature leaf drop.

Crop rotation helps limit its spread.

🌻
Sunflower

Alternaria Blight

Creates dark spots on leaves, stems, and the flower disc, spreading quickly in dense sowing.

Certified seed and proper spacing reduce the risk.

🌾
Barley

Barley Rust

Creates orange-brown pustules on leaves, which weaken grain filling when severe.

Rust-resistant varieties are the most reliable protection.

🍆
Brinjal

Fruit & Stem Blight

Creates dark, sunken lesions on fruit and stems that spread quickly in humid weather.

Removing infected parts promptly helps stop the spread.

🫛
Okra

Yellow Vein Mosaic Virus

Leaf veins turn yellow and growth stalls; whitefly spreads this virus.

Timely whitefly control limits its spread.

🥦
Cauliflower & cabbage

Black Rot

Creates V-shaped yellow spots at the leaf margins that later turn black.

Clean seed and crop rotation help prevent it.

🟡
Turmeric

Rhizome Rot

Excess soil moisture causes the rhizomes to rot and the plant to wilt.

Good field drainage is the most important preventive measure.

🫚
Ginger

Rhizome Rot

Rot in the roots and rhizomes turns the plant yellow and wilted, especially in waterlogged fields.

Sowing on raised beds improves drainage.

🌴
Papaya

Papaya Ringspot Virus

Mosaic spots on leaves and stunted growth are common symptoms; aphids spread it.

Aphid control and disease-resistant varieties are most effective.

🔴
Pomegranate

Bacterial Blight

Creates dark, water-soaked spots on leaves, fruit, and branches that may later crack open.

Pruning infected branches and disinfecting tools prevents spread.

🍏
Apple

Apple Scab

Creates velvety, dark brown spots on leaves and fruit; most common in humid spring weather.

Removing fallen infected leaves reduces infection the following season.

🌸
Chrysanthemum

Leaf Spot

Creates round, dark spots on leaves that enlarge and can merge together.

Avoiding overhead watering slows the spread.

🌼
Jasmine

Powdery Mildew

Creates a white, powdery coating on leaves and buds, reducing flower quality.

Pruning to improve air circulation helps reduce infection.

🌷
Lily

Botrytis Blight (Gray Mold)

Creates a gray fungal coating on petals and stems in cool, humid weather.

Regularly removing spent flowers and improving air circulation helps.

🎋
Sugarcane

Red Rot

Creates red streaks inside the stem and weakens the plant from within.

Using disease-free seed cane is the most essential precaution.
